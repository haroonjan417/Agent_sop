SOP-804: Procedure for Receiving Damaged Goods
1. Quarantine the affected shipment immediately in Bay Q.
2. Take high-resolution photo evidence of exterior packaging and damaged items.
3. Issue a Non-Conformance Report (NCR) to the supplier within 24 hours.
4. Notify QA Lead and Warehouse Supervisor via internal ticket.
5. Log partial return in ERP under Reason Code: DAMAGED_ON_ARRIVAL.
